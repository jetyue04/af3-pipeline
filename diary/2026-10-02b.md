## Update — Motif subdomain results (same session)

Both motifs failed to bind RAB5A independently. Full results:

| Run | iptm | pair_iptm | PAE_min | Verdict |
|-----|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Motif 1 (72aa) + RAB5A GTP | 0.62 | 0.23 | 12.8 Å | No interface |
| Motif 1 (72aa) + RAB5A GDP | 0.57 | 0.16 | 13.0 Å | No interface |
| Motif 2 (62aa) + RAB5A GTP | 0.63 | 0.19 | 14.8 Å | No interface |
| Motif 2 (62aa) + RAB5A GDP | 0.61 | 0.17 | 16.9 Å | No interface |

chain_iptm[A] (motif self-confidence): Motif1=0.17, Motif2=0.12 — both disordered outside HB context.

**Conclusion: The HB domain is the minimal binding unit. Both motifs are required simultaneously — cooperative/bivalent binding surface. Neither patch is independently sufficient.**

GTP-selectivity also disappears in isolated motifs (delta pair_iptm ≈ 0.02–0.07 vs 0.33 for full HB domain), confirming the Switch II contact geometry only forms when both patches are present together.

**Implication for drug discovery:** Target the groove between the two contact patches on RAB5A, not either patch alone.
