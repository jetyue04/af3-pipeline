## What we decided today

### New scientific goal
Expand the existing FAM129B/RAB5A pipeline to include a **TRPA1 Human antagonist screen**.
Goal: identify novel small molecules that block the TRPA1 ion channel (pain/inflammation target).

### Strategy chosen
We evaluated two approaches and chose a hybrid:

1. **Primary screen: AutoDock Vina against experimental cryo-EM structure (PDB 6V9W)**
   - 6V9W is human TRPA1 at 3.0 Å co-crystallized with antagonist A-967079
   - The co-crystallized ligand defines the exact antagonist binding pocket (S4–S5 linker / S6)
   - No cluster time needed for the main screen — runs locally
   - Screen ~5–10K compounds from Enamine ion-channel focused set

2. **Secondary validation: AF3 co-folding for top 20 Vina hits**
   - TRPA1 TM domain construct (residues 682–1119, 438 aa) + candidate ligand
   - 3 AF3 model seeds per compound — consistent pose across seeds = high confidence
   - Hits passing both Vina/GNINA AND AF3 iptm > 0.5 are the final shortlist for ordering

**Reason for not using AF3 as the primary screen:** AF3 co-folding = one cluster job per compound.
For thousands of compounds this is impractical. Vina is orders of magnitude faster for large libraries.
AF3 is most valuable as a final validation step for a small set of shortlisted candidates.

## Context / background

TRPA1 (Transient Receptor Potential Ankyrin 1) is a homotetrameric ion channel
expressed in sensory neurons. It is activated by:
- Cold temperatures (< 17°C)
- Reactive electrophiles (allicin, cinnamaldehyde, acrolein)
- Oxidative stress (H₂O₂, 4-HNE)

Antagonists block channel opening and have potential therapeutic use in:
- Chronic pain
- Neurogenic asthma/cough
- Itch/atopic dermatitis

The primary antagonist binding site is in the transmembrane domain between the
S4–S5 linker and S6 helix, accessible from the intracellular face. This is
the target of HC-030031, A-967079, GRC 17536, and most published small-molecule antagonists.

The existing `af3-pipeline` infrastructure (Vina screen, GNINA rescore, triage,
receptor prep) is reused directly — no new code needed for Phase 0 and Phase 1.
